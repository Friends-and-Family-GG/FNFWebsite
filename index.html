About the Game

Friday Night Funkin' is a rhythm-based indie game originally created for Ludum Dare 47. Help Boyfriend win rap battles against a growing cast of opponents to prove his worth and win the heart of Girlfriend.

How to Play

Arrow Keys

Use the arrow keys (or WASD) to hit notes in time with the music.

Stay on Beat

Match the incoming arrows as they reach the target zone.

Don't Miss!

Missing too many notes will drain your health bar and end the song.
